# ADR-007: AIOS + Digital Twin as Core Runtime Pillars

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-05

## Problem
NANFO needs a consistent architecture for explainable intelligence and spatial operational context.

## Context
Traditional NMS tools are reactive and spatially blind; NANFO differentiates through explainable AI and living digital twin workflows.

## Options
1. Keep AI and visualization as add-on modules.
2. Treat AIOS and Digital Twin as first-class architecture pillars with shared canonical object identity.

## Decision
Adopt AIOS and Digital Twin as core architecture pillars with synchronized object identity and event-driven updates.

## Consequences
- Pros: coherent reasoning-to-visualization loop, stronger operator trust and diagnosis quality.
- Cons: higher design complexity at early stages.
